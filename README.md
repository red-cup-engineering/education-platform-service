Theme-neutral education kernel: course compilation, FSRS-lite scheduling, light-cone progression, checkers, **model-estimated recall**, sessions, persistence, and browser projection. Its optional `practice-evidence` contract preserves locally observed first-try versus post-feedback review and the time since previous practice, without claiming an independent competence assessment or a credential. A passed gate is a study event, not evidence of successful transfer into a novel real-world job.
